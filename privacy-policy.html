NEON SPRINT: CYBER PROTOCOL

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Neon Sprint: Cyber Protocol. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any data you share with us. This policy outlines how we handle information related to our game services, including gameplay, virtual economy, and in-app transactions.

2. Services Offered

Neon Sprint: Cyber Protocol is an endless runner game featuring:

  • Nanite Economy: A virtual currency system where players collect "Nanites" to sustain gameplay.
  • Cyber Vault: An in-game store allowing players to purchase Nanite packs to extend playtime once free limits are reached.
  • Player Accounts: A local account system to track high scores, distance, and Nanite balances.

3. Data Collection

  • Local Data: Game progress, high scores, and virtual currency balances are stored locally on your device or in your browser's cache.
  • Account Information: If you create a player account, your username and password hash are stored to manage your virtual vault.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not track your location, contacts, or browsing history.

4. In-App Purchases & Payments

To provide real-money transaction services for Nanite packs, we use Razorpay, a secure third-party payment gateway. When you make a purchase:

  • We do not collect or store your credit card or bank details on our servers.
  • Transaction data (Email, Contact, and Payment ID) is processed securely by Razorpay to confirm the success of your deposit and credit your Cyber Vault.

5. Security

We implement security protocols to protect your local data and ensure that payment interfaces are accessed via encrypted channels. User sessions are automatically cleared upon app exit for maximum security.
